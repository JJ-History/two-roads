Two Histories · One Enduring Question

Two Roads
To Freedom

Two peoples walked out of bondage—one crossed a sea, the other crossed a river in a storm. This exhibit compares the Exodus from Egypt with the American struggle for independence, drawing on David McCullough’s 1776 to explore leadership, danger, endurance, and the meaning of freedom.

The Question

What does it take for a people to seek freedom—and what happens after the crossing?

A Note on Comparison

These events belong to very different times and circumstances. The parallels are a way to ask questions, not to claim that the histories are the same.
